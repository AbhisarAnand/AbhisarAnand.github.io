Supplier forecast automation

Generate the workbooks, then make review straightforward

The problem

Preparing and validating individual supplier forecasts could take two to three weeks. A readiness assessment also surfaced gaps in regular forecast communication.

My work

I built a tool that takes the supplier list and source data, generates each supplier’s Excel forecast in horizontal and vertical layouts, and prepares Outlook drafts with the right recipients and copied contacts.

How it works

Generation and sending are separate steps. The tool prepares the files and drafts; people validate the output before sending it. That keeps the monthly communication process reviewable.

What changed

Workbook generation takes under a minute, followed by a few hours of validation. The team adopted the workflow for monthly forecasts.

Scope

The full process includes human validation and sending. The tool is not described as sending forecasts autonomously.
