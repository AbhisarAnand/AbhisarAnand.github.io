Purchase price variance dashboard

From weekly file preparation to part-level investigation

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • DuckDB
  • Data visualization
See the workflow ↓

The problem

3–4 hours of preparation → under 30 seconds

A weekly report depended on one colleague spending roughly three to four hours preparing and consolidating source data. Other people had to wait for that step before reviewing purchase price variance.

My contribution

I built a source-file consolidation pipeline and integrated it into a dashboard used by the sourcing team. Users can move from a global view to a commodity, buyer, supplier, organization, or individual part.

From input to outcome

How it works

Exception reporting highlights records that need investigation, including parts with a zero standard cost. The same interface shows favorable and unfavorable variance and progress against goals.

  1. 01

    Consolidate source files

    The pipeline brings source data together into a form the team can use, replacing repeated manual preparation.

  2. 02

    Review the global picture

    The dashboard exposes favorable and unfavorable purchase price variance and progress against the team’s goals.

  3. 03

    Find the exceptions

    Part-level checks surface records such as zero-standard-cost items that need investigation before the numbers can be trusted.

  4. 04

    Drill into the cause

    Users can explore by commodity, buyer, supplier, organization or part and bring the relevant records into a follow-up discussion.

Behind the implementation

Design decisions

Start with the business bottleneck

The original problem was the time the team spent waiting for prepared data. I built consolidation and exploration into the same workflow so a faster calculation also became easier to use.

Put data quality beside the metric

A favorable or unfavorable total is only useful if the underlying records make sense. Exception reporting gives the team a concrete place to investigate and correct questionable entries.

What changed

The consolidation step runs in under 30 seconds. Exception reporting surfaces approximately 10–15 parts per month for investigation and correction.

Project context

These figures describe the internal workflow I worked on. Consolidation time excludes subsequent investigation and review.
