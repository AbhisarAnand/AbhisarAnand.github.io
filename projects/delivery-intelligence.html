On-time delivery intelligence

Explore global delivery data and possible disruption

The problem

Teams needed a common view of delivery performance and a way to investigate gaps across suppliers and shipments.

My work

I built the dashboard and data workflow, combining unstructured source data into a global delivery view with drill-down. I also added news-based exploration of possible disruption and a conversational agent for questions and report generation.

How it works

The news feature helps users explore which suppliers or shipments may be exposed to an event. The conversational interface gives another way to work with the underlying delivery data and prepare reports.

What changed

I demonstrated the system across teams, including to the CIO and sourcing leadership.

Scope

The news feature helps people investigate potential exposure. It is not an automated decision to reroute shipments or change suppliers.
