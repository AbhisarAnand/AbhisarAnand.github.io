Supplier readiness assessment

Find where a production ramp needs more preparation

  • Survey design

See the workflow ↓

The problem

A recurring assessment across about 80 suppliers

Ahead of a production ramp, the sourcing team needed a consistent way to understand supplier readiness and decide where to focus follow-up.

My contribution

I built a survey sent to about 80 suppliers and tools that process the responses into a readiness view. The team can identify lagging suppliers and direct commodity managers to the areas that need attention.

From input to outcome

How it works

The workflow connects supplier responses to a repeatable review process, so the team can run the assessment again rather than rebuild the analysis each time.

  1. 01

    Ask about readiness

    The survey collects critical information about whether suppliers are prepared for the planned production ramp.

  2. 02

    Process the responses

    Analysis tools turn responses into a consistent view rather than leaving the team to compare each response manually.

  3. 03

    Identify follow-up

    The team can identify lagging suppliers and give commodity managers a clearer starting point for engagement.

  4. 04

    Repeat the assessment

    The survey and analysis are reused as a recurring process so readiness can be reviewed again as plans change.

Behind the implementation

Design decisions

Make the output actionable

The point of the assessment is to guide where the team spends its attention. The response analysis connects the survey to supplier follow-up rather than stopping at a summary of answers.

Build a repeatable process

The recurring workflow lets the team reassess readiness without rebuilding the analysis from the beginning. Approximately 80 describes survey recipients, not a claimed response rate.

What changed

The survey and analysis became a recurring team process.

Project context

About 80 refers to suppliers sent the survey. It is not a claim about response rate or improved delivery performance.
