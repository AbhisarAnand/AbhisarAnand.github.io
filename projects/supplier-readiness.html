Supplier readiness assessment

Find where a production ramp needs more preparation

The problem

Ahead of a production ramp, the sourcing team needed a consistent way to understand supplier readiness and decide where to focus follow-up.

My work

I built a survey sent to about 80 suppliers and tools that process the responses into a readiness view. The team can identify lagging suppliers and direct commodity managers to the areas that need attention.

How it works

The workflow connects supplier responses to a repeatable review process, so the team can run the assessment again rather than rebuild the analysis each time.

What changed

The survey and analysis became a recurring team process.

Scope

About 80 refers to suppliers sent the survey. It is not a claim about response rate or improved delivery performance.
